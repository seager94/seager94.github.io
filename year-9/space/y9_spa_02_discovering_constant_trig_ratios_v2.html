1Why this matters

A real situation that is secretly today's mathematics.

A shed company sells three sizes of garden shed: small, medium and large. Every shed has the same roof pitch (the angle the roof makes with the horizontal), but bigger sheds have longer roofs.

The rafter timber costs $12.50 per metre. The estimator never measures a rafter. She measures the rise of the roof and doubles it. Small shed: rise 1 m, rafter 2 m. Medium: rise 1.5 m, rafter 3 m. Large: rise 2 m, rafter 4 m.

Why does one shortcut work for every size of shed?

For the roof angle θ, the rise is the opposite side and the rafter is the hypotenuse. On every shed, rise ÷ rafter = 0.5. Today you discover why: when the angle stays the same, the ratio of two sides stays the same, however big the triangle is.

💡 Every length is multiplied by 3, so 3 cm becomes 9 cm and 5 cm becomes 15 cm. But 9 ÷ 15 = 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6: the scale factor appears on the top and the bottom, so it cancels. Angles and side ratios never change under enlargement.

5Worked example — the 3-step method

Three right-angled triangles are nested inside each other on a grid. They all share the angle θ at A. Do their side ratios depend on the size of the triangle?

1

Explain the pattern and shrink to hypotenuse 1
The triangles are similar: ADE is ABC enlarged by 2, AFG by 3. Each ratio is (k × side) ÷ (k × side), so the scale factor k cancels. The ratios belong to the angle, not to the triangle. Shrink ABC by scale factor 15 and its hypotenuse is 1 unit: the opposite side is then 0.6 and the adjacent side is 0.8, exactly the two ratios.
Unit-hypotenuse triangle for this θ:   opposite = 0.6, adjacent = 0.8, hypotenuse = 1

Correct. 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6 and 9 ÷ 15 = 0.6. Enlarging multiplies the top and the bottom of the ratio by the same scale factor, so the ratio stays the same for that angle.
That's thinking additively. The sides grew by multiplying, not adding. Work out 9 ÷ 15 and compare it with 3 ÷ 5.
Enlargement never changes angles. Similar triangles have exactly the same angles; only the lengths change. That is why their side ratios match.
