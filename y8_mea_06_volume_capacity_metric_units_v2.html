1Why this matters

A real situation that's secretly today's measurement challenge.

You've just bought a new fish tank. The pet shop says it needs at least 180 litres of water to keep the fish healthy. Your tank measures:

90 cm long × 40 cm wide × 50 cm deep

Can the tank hold enough? You need its capacity in litres — but the dimensions are in centimetres. To get there, you need to understand how cubic centimetres relate to millilitres, and then millilitres to litres.

The big idea today:

1 cm³ holds exactly 1 mL of water. That one fact connects volume to capacity.

From there, the metric ladder takes you the rest of the way:
1000 mL = 1 L
1 000 000 cm³ = 1 m³

The tricky part: converting cubic units is not the same as converting lengths. You'll discover exactly why.

3The 1 cm³ = 1 mL connection, and the cubic conversion trap

The diagram shows why converting cubic units requires cubing the length conversion factor — not just using it directly.

Reading the diagram
Large cube = 1 m³ (each edge = 1 m)
Small cube = 1 cm³ = 1 mL

Why 1 000 000 and not 100?

The length conversion is 1 m = 100 cm. But volume fills three directions, so you must cube the factor:

100 × 100 × 100 = 1003 = 1 000 000

✗ Named misconception:
Linear-versus-cubic conversion-factor error
Applying the length factor (×100) to a cubic unit gives an answer that is 10 000 times too small.

5Worked examples — four key conversion types

We'll work through: (A) cm³ ↔ mL, (B) mL ↔ L, (C) cm³ ↔ m³, (D) a practical fish-tank problem.

A
Cubic centimetres to/from millilitres
Since 1 cm³ = 1 mL exactly, the number stays the same — only the label on the unit changes.
Convert 450 cm³ to mL:
450 cm³ = 450 mL

Convert 3200 mL to cm³:
3200 mL = 3200 cm³

Key fact: cm³ and mL are interchangeable — the number is always identical.
B
Millilitres to/from litres
There are 1000 mL in every litre. Divide to go up to litres; multiply to go back down.
Convert 2500 mL to litres:
2500 ÷ 1000 = 2.5 L

Convert 0.75 L to mL:
0.75 × 1000 = 750 mL
C
Cubic centimetres to/from cubic metres
Length: 1 m = 100 cm. Volume (three dimensions): 100³ = 1 000 000. So 1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³.
Convert 5 000 000 cm³ to m³:
5 000 000 ÷ 1 000 000 = 5 m³

Convert 2.4 m³ to cm³:
2.4 × 1 000 000 = 2 400 000 cm³

⚠ The conversion factor is 1 000 000, not 100. Using 100 is the linear-versus-cubic error.
D
Fish tank capacity in litres
Find volume in cm³ first, then convert step by step: cm³ → mL (same number) → L (÷ 1000).
Tank: 90 cm × 40 cm × 50 cm

Step 1 — Volume: 90 × 40 × 50 = 180 000 cm³
Step 2 — cm³ to mL: 180 000 cm³ = 180 000 mL
Step 3 — mL to L: 180 000 ÷ 1000 = 180 L
Answer The tank holds exactly 180 L — just enough for the fish!
