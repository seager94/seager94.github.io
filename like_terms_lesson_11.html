1Warm-up — what belongs together?

Before we deal with algebra, think about everyday sorting.

Suppose you have these items in your school bag:

  • 3 apples 🍎🍎🍎
  • 2 bananas 🍌🍌
  • 4 apples 🍎🍎🍎🍎
  • 1 banana 🍌

If someone asks "how much fruit do you have?", you naturally group apples with apples and bananas with bananas: 7 apples and 3 bananas. You don't say "10 fruit-things" and you definitely don't say "5 apple-banana-mixtures."

Algebra works the same way. The expression 3a + 2b + 4a + b simplifies to 7a + 3b — we group the a's together and the b's together. Today we learn the rules for doing this with any algebraic expression.

2Key vocabulary

Tap each card to flip and reveal the definition. You'll use these five words constantly today.

Constant
Tap to flip
A term with no variable — just a number.
In 4x − 7: the constant is −7.
Variable
Tap to flip
A letter that represents an unknown value.
In 7k − 2: the variable is k.
Coefficient
Tap to flip
The number that multiplies a variable — it sits in front.
In −5y: the coefficient is −5. In x: the coefficient is 1.
Term
Tap to flip
A single number, variable, or product separated by + or − signs.
In 3x + 5: the terms are 3x and 5.
Like terms
Tap to flip
Terms with the same variable to the same power. Constants are like terms with each other.
3x and −7x are like terms. 3x and 3x² are not.

5Worked example — the 3-step method

Simplify: 14x + 9 − 2x + 8 − 5x + 1

1
Identify like terms
Colour-code the x-terms and the constants.
14x + 9 − 2x + 8 − 5x + 1
2
Combine the coefficients of each group
Add the coefficients of like terms. Keep the variable unchanged.
(14 − 2 − 5)x + (9 + 8 + 1)
= 7x + 18
3
Check by substituting a value
If x = 2: the original is 14(2) + 9 − 2(2) + 8 − 5(2) + 1 = 28 + 9 − 4 + 8 − 10 + 1 = 32. The simplified form gives 7(2) + 18 = 14 + 18 = 32. ✓
Both expressions give 32 when x = 2 — the simplification is correct.
Answer 7x + 18
