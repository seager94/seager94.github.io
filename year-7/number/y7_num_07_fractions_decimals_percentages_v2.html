1Why this matters

A real situation that's secretly today's maths.

Imagine you walk into three different shops. Each has a sign on the front window:

  • Shop A: "14 OFF EVERYTHING"
  • Shop B: "0.25 OFF EVERY DOLLAR"
  • Shop C: "25% OFF STOREWIDE"

Which shop has the best deal? Trick question — they're all the same offer, written three different ways.

Fractions, decimals, and percentages are three languages for the same quantity. The number doesn't change — only the way we write it. Today you'll learn to switch fluently between them, starting with six benchmark equivalences you should know on sight.

2Key vocabulary

Tap each card to flip and reveal the definition. You'll use these five words constantly today.

Fraction
Tap to flip
A number written as parts out of a whole, with a numerator above and denominator below.
In 38: numerator is 3, denominator is 8.
Decimal
Tap to flip
A number using place value to the right of a decimal point — tenths, hundredths, thousandths.
0.375 means 3 tenths + 7 hundredths + 5 thousandths.
Percent
Tap to flip
A fraction with denominator 100. The symbol % literally means "out of 100".
37% = 37100.
Equivalent
Tap to flip
Two numbers are equivalent if they have the same value, even if written differently.
12, 0.5, and 50% are all equivalent.
Terminating
Tap to flip
A decimal that stops after a finite number of digits. The opposite is "repeating" — the digits go on forever.
38 = 0.375 terminates. 13 = 0.333... repeats.

5Worked example — convert 38 to all three forms

Use the triangle below to picture the moves. Each edge is one conversion rule.

All three boxes are the same number — only the form changes.
1
Fraction → Decimal: divide
"Out of 8" means divide the numerator by the denominator. So 3 ÷ 8.
38  =  3 ÷ 8  =  0.375
2
Decimal → Percent: × 100
"Percent" means "out of 100", so multiplying by 100 moves the decimal point two places right.
0.375  ×  100  =  37.5%
3
Sense-check against benchmarks
We expect 38 to sit between 14 (= 25%) and 12 (= 50%). Is 37.5% between 25% and 50%? Yes ✓
25% < 37.5% < 50%  ✓ — the answer is reasonable.
Answer 38  =  0.375  =  37.5%
