1Why this matters

A real situation that's secretly today's algebra.

At the school fete you find a bowling stall. The sign reads:

  • $4 entry fee
  • $2 per ball you bowl

You bowl until your money runs out — your total bill is $18. How many balls did you bowl?

You could guess and check, but with the right method you can find the answer directly.

Call the number of balls b. Your bill is 2b + 4 = 18. Today we learn how to solve equations like this in just a few short, careful steps — and how to verify our answer is right. We finish every problem the same way: substitute the answer back into the original equation and check that both sides are equal.

Equation

A statement that two expressions are equal. It always contains an equals sign.
3x + 7 = 19 is an equation.
Solution
Tap to flip
The value of the variable that makes the equation true.
The solution of 3x + 7 = 19 is x = 4.
Inverse operation
Tap to flip
The operation that undoes another. + and − are inverses. × and ÷ are inverses.
The inverse of "+ 7" is "− 7". The inverse of "× 3" is "÷ 3".
Both sides
Tap to flip
An equation is like a balanced scale. Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other — or the balance is lost.
Subtract 7 from one side? Subtract 7 from the other side too.
Verify
Tap to flip
To check your solution by substituting it back into the original equation and showing both sides are equal.
If x = 4 solves 3x + 7 = 19, check: 3(4) + 7 = 19 ✓

5Worked example — the 3-step method

Solve: 3x + 7 = 19

1
Identify what's being done to x
Read the LHS: x is multiplied by 3, then 7 is added. To undo, work in reverse — undo the "+ 7" first, then undo the "× 3".
3x + 7 = 19
First undo: + 7 (use −7). Then undo: × 3 (use ÷3).
2
Apply the inverse operation to both sides
Subtract 7 from both sides, then divide both sides by 3. Show every line.
3x + 7 = 19original
3x + 7 − 7 = 19 − 7subtract 7 both sides
3x = 12simplify
3x3 = 123divide by 3 both sides
x = 4simplify
3
Verify — substitute x = 4 back into the original equation
This is non-negotiable. Always verify. If the two sides aren't equal, you've made a mistake somewhere — go back and find it.
LHS = 3(4) + 7substitute x = 4
      = 12 + 7multiply first
      = 19add
RHS = 19
LHS = RHS ✓solution confirmed
Verified: both sides equal 19 when x = 4, so x = 4 is the solution.
Answer x = 4
