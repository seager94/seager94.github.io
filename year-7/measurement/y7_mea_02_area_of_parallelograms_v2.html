1Why this matters — the rearrangement trick

A parallelogram doesn't look like a rectangle. But with one clever cut, it becomes one.

Imagine a parallelogram-shaped patch of grass. Slice off the right-hand triangle with a straight vertical cut, and slide that triangle all the way to the left. The slanted left edge of the parallelogram lines up exactly with the slanted edge of the moved triangle.

What you're left with is a rectangle with the same base and the same perpendicular height as the parallelogram you started with.

So the parallelogram has the same area as that rectangle:

Area of a parallelogram A = base × perpendicular height
→slide left
⚠️ Watch out: like triangles last lesson, the misconception risk is the same — students sometimes multiply the base by the slanted side instead of the perpendicular height. The slanted side is too long. Always use the perpendicular height.

2Key vocabulary

Tap each card to flip and reveal the definition. The two height words are the ones to get right today.

Parallelogram
Tap to flip
A four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides. Opposite sides are equal in length.
A rectangle is a special parallelogram (all angles are 90°).
Base
Tap to flip
The side you choose to measure from — usually the bottom. Any side can be the base.
If the base is the bottom, height is measured straight up to the opposite side.
Perpendicular height
Tap to flip
The straight-line distance from the base to the opposite side, measured at right angles. This is what we multiply by the base.
Often shown as a dashed line inside the parallelogram.
Slanted side
Tap to flip
The actual sloped side of the parallelogram. Always longer than the perpendicular height. Do NOT use this in the area formula.
If you multiply base × slanted side, the answer will be too big.
Area formula
Tap to flip
A = base × perpendicular height. Units: cm², m², mm². Always perpendicular height — never the slanted side.
b = 8 cm, h = 5 cm → A = 8 × 5 = 40 cm².

5Worked example — base 12 cm, perpendicular height 7 cm

Find the area of this parallelogram.

1
Identify the base and the perpendicular height
The base sits along the bottom — 12 cm. The perpendicular height is the dashed line drawn inside at right angles — 7 cm.
base = 12 cm  ·  perpendicular height = 7 cm
2
Apply A = base × perpendicular height
Multiply the two values. Use the perpendicular height (the dashed line), not any slanted side.
A = 12 × 7
A = 84
3
Write the answer with the correct unit
Lengths were in centimetres, so area is in square centimetres (cm²).
A = 84 cm²
Answer A = 84 cm²
✓ Correct

Using the perpendicular height

A = base × perpendicular height
A = 12 × 7 = 84 cm²
The 7 cm is measured at a right angle from the base to the opposite side. This matches the rectangle we'd get after the rearrangement.
✗ Common mistake

Using the slanted side

A = base × slanted side
A = 12 × 9 = 108 cm² ✗
The slanted side (9 cm) is longer than the perpendicular height. Multiplying by it gives a value that's too big — the answer doesn't match the rearranged rectangle.
