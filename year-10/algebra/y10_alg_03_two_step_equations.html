1Warm-up — think of a number

A puzzle to start. Try it in your head before reading the explanation.

🎲 I'm thinking of a number. I multiply it by 4, then subtract 3. The answer is 17. What was my number?

Most students get this in their head: the answer is 5. But how did you find it?

You almost certainly worked backwards — you added 3 to undo the subtraction (getting 20), then divided by 4 to undo the multiplication (getting 5). You did the inverse operations in reverse order.

That mental routine is the algebra. If we call the mystery number x, the puzzle is the equation 4x − 3 = 17 — and you just solved your first two-step equation.

Forward — what was done to x
x multiply× 4 → 4x subtract− 3 → 17
Reverse — how we solve
x = 5 ← divide÷ 4 4x = 20 ← add+ 3 17
💡 The reverse path is the mirror image of the forward path. Last-on, first-off — like taking shoes off before socks.

2Key vocabulary

Most of these you've met before — two-step equation and reverse order are the new ones. Tap each card to flip.

Inverse operation
Tap to flip
The operation that undoes another. Pairs: + ↔ −, × ↔ ÷.
The inverse of × 4 is ÷ 4. The inverse of − 3 is + 3.
Two-step equation
Tap to flip
An equation where two operations have been applied to the variable. Needs two inverse steps to solve.
4x − 3 = 17 → ×4 and −3 both done to x.
Reverse order
Tap to flip
Undo the operations in the opposite order they were applied. +/− comes off first; ×/÷ comes off second.
For 4x − 3: undo − 3 first, then × 4.
Coefficient
Tap to flip
The number that multiplies the variable. The last thing to "peel off."
In 4x − 3: the coefficient is 4. In −x + 7: it's −1.
Constant term
Tap to flip
The plain number stuck on the variable side. The first thing we move across.
In 4x − 3 = 17: the constant on the LHS is −3.

5Worked example — the 3-step method

Solve: 4x − 7 = 13

1
Identify what's been done to x — and plan the reverse
Two operations have been applied: multiplied by 4, then subtracted 7. To undo, work in reverse order: first add 7, then divide by 4.
Forward (what was done)
x ×4 → 4x −7 → 13
Reverse (how we solve) — peel +/− first
x ← ÷4 4x ← +7 13
2
Undo the +/− first — add 7 to both sides
Whatever we do to one side, we do to the other. Keep the equation balanced.
4x − 7 = 13
4x − 7 + 7 = 13 + 7 add 7 to both sides
4x = 20
3
Undo the ×/÷ second — divide both sides by 4
Now it's a one-step equation. Divide by the coefficient to isolate x. Finish with a substitution check.
4x = 20
4x4 = 204 divide both sides by 4
x = 5
Check: 4(5) − 7 = 20 − 7 = 13 ✓
Answer x = 5
