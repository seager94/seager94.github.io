2The five index laws

Tap each card to flip and check the rule. These are the same five laws you met last term — read them like a refresher, not a re-teach.

Law 1
Multiplication
Tap to flip
Same base — add the indices.
xa × xb = xa+b
x3 × x5 = x8
Law 2
Division
Tap to flip
Same base — subtract the indices.
xa ÷ xb = xa−b
x7 ÷ x2 = x5
Law 3
Power of a power
Tap to flip
Bracket raised to another power — multiply the indices.
(xa)b = xa×b
(x4)3 = x12
Law 4
Zero index
Tap to flip
Anything (not zero) raised to the power 0 equals 1.
x0 = 1
70 = 1  ·  k0 = 1
Law 5
Negative index
Tap to flip
A negative index means take the reciprocal.
x−n = 1xn
x−2 = 1x2

5Worked example — combining two laws

Simplify: 2x5 × 3x26x3

1
Apply the multiplication law to the numerator
Same base × same base → multiply coefficients, add indices.
2x5 × 3x2 = (2 × 3) × (x5 × x2)
= 6 × x5+2 = 6x7
2
Now apply the division law
The expression is now 6x76x3. Divide coefficients, subtract indices.
6x76x3 = (6 ÷ 6) × (x7 ÷ x3)
= 1 × x7−3 = x4
3
Verify by substituting a value
Pick x = 2. Both forms should give the same number.
Original: 2(32) × 3(4)6(8) = 64 × 1248 = 76848 = 16
Simplified: x4 = 24 = 16 ✓
Answer x4
