1Warm-up — pay day at the café

You already know how to substitute. Today we work in reverse.

You've got a casual job earning $25 per hour. If you work h hours, your gross pay P is:

P = 25h

This week you worked 6 hours. What's your pay?
Substitute h = 6:   P = 25 × 6 = $150 ✓
That's substitution — you knew the letter, you found the answer.

Next week you check your bank: $175 was deposited. How many hours did you work?
Now you need to solve:   25h = 175,   h = ?
That's an equation — you know the answer, you need to find the letter.

Today's big idea
Substitution gives you the answer when you know the letter.

Equations are the reverse — you find the letter when you know the answer.

2Key vocabulary

Tap each card to flip and reveal the definition. These five words are the language of equation-solving.

Equation
Tap to flip
A statement that two expressions are equal — uses an = sign.
25h = 175 means "25 times h gives 175".
Variable
Tap to flip
The letter standing for the unknown value we need to find.
In 25h = 175, the variable is h (the hours worked).
Solution
Tap to flip
The value of the variable that makes the equation true when substituted in.
h = 7 solves 25h = 175, because 25 × 7 = 175.
Substitute
Tap to flip
Replace the variable with a specific number to check or evaluate.
Substitute h = 7 into 25h: 25 × 7 = 175 ✓
Inverse operation
Tap to flip
The operation that undoes another. Subtract undoes add; divide undoes multiply.
To undo × 25, we use ÷ 25.

5Worked example — the 3-step method

ScenarioYou sold raffle tickets at $3 each for the school fundraiser. Total takings: $21. How many tickets did you sell?
Letting t = tickets sold, the equation is   3t = 21. Solve for t.
1
Identify the inverse operation
What is being done to t? It is being multiplied by 3. The inverse of × 3 is ÷ 3.
3t = 21    ↑ multiply by 3  ⟶  undo with ÷ 3
2
Apply the inverse to both sides
Whatever we do to one side, we must do to the other — that keeps the equation balanced.
3t ÷ 3 = 21 ÷ 3
t = 7
3
Verify by substitution
Replace t with 7 in the original equation. Does the left side equal the right side?
3 × 7 = 21 ✓
Both sides equal 21 — the substitution is true, so t = 7 is the solution.
Answer t = 7  (you sold 7 tickets)
