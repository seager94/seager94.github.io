1Why this matters

A real situation where time zones matter — right now, in your life.

You want to video-call a friend who has just moved to London. They text you: "I'm free at 6 pm my time — what time is that for you in Adelaide?"

Without knowing how time zones work, you could miss the call entirely — or ring them in the middle of the night. Airlines, businesses, and sports broadcasters face exactly this challenge every single day.

This lesson gives you a reliable method: find the UTC offset for each place, calculate the difference, then add or subtract it in the right direction.

5Worked example — Adelaide to Perth

It is 2:00 pm (14:00) in Adelaide (UTC+9:30). What time is it in Perth (UTC+8)?

Adelaide (UTC+9:30)
14:00 (2:00 pm)
→
Perth (UTC+8)
12:30 (12:30 pm)
⚠️ Central misconception: applying a zone offset in the wrong direction. Moving EAST (higher UTC number) = later time = ADD the offset difference. Moving WEST (lower UTC number) = earlier time = SUBTRACT the offset difference. Always ask: "Is the destination east or west of where I'm starting?"

The rule: Target time = Source time + (Target offset − Source offset)
1
Find the offsets
Identify the UTC offset for each city and determine direction.
Adelaide = UTC+9:30     Perth = UTC+8
Perth has a lower offset → Perth is WEST of Adelaide → Perth time is BEHIND (earlier than) Adelaide.
2
Calculate the difference and apply
Find the offset difference, then subtract (going west = earlier time).
Offset difference = 9:30 − 8:00 = 1 hour 30 minutes
Perth is 1h 30min BEHIND Adelaide → subtract
Perth time = 14:00 − 1h 30min = 12:30
3
State the answer and verify
Write the answer, then sense-check: Perth has a smaller UTC number, so its time should be earlier than Adelaide's.
Answer: 12:30 in Perth.
Check: Perth (UTC+8) has a lower UTC offset than Adelaide (UTC+9:30), so Perth time must be earlier. 12:30 < 14:00 ✓
Summary When it is 2:00 pm in Adelaide, it is 12:30 pm in Perth.

6Spot the mistake

A student converted the time from Adelaide to Suva. They made one error — click the line where the mistake first appears, then write the correction.

It is 10:00 am (10:00) in Adelaide (UTC+9:30). Find the time in Suva, Fiji (UTC+12).
1 Adelaide = UTC+9:30, Suva = UTC+12
2 Suva is EAST of Adelaide, so Suva is AHEAD of Adelaide
3 Difference = 12:00 − 9:30 = 2 hours 30 minutes
4 Suva time = 10:00 − 2:30 = 07:30
5 Suva time = 07:30

✓ Right — that's where the mistake first appears.

Why: Suva is east of (and ahead of) Adelaide. When travelling east, you ADD the offset difference. The student subtracted instead, giving a time before Adelaide's time — which is wrong for a zone that is supposed to be ahead. Correct calculation: 10:00 + 2:30 = 12:30.
