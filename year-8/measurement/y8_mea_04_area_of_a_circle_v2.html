1Why this matters

Real situations where area of a circle saves money and solves problems.

A farmer wants to water a circular paddock using a centre-pivot sprinkler. The arm extends 40 metres from the centre. How much ground does it actually water?

A pizza restaurant advertises a "large" pizza with a 30 cm diameter. A competitor offers an "extra-large" at 34 cm. Is the extra-large really worth the extra price?

Both questions need the same calculation: how much flat space does a circle cover?

The answer always involves the radius — not the diameter.

The formula is A = πr².

Today you will see why this formula works (using a clever dissection argument) — then practise using it in every direction.

2Key vocabulary

Tap each card to flip it and reveal the definition. You will use all five terms today.

Radius
Tap to flip
The distance from the centre of a circle to any point on its edge. Labelled r.
A circle with r = 5 cm has its edge exactly 5 cm from the centre all the way around.
Diameter
Tap to flip
A straight line through the centre joining opposite edges. Labelled d. Always equal to 2r.
If d = 12 cm, then r = 6 cm. Halving first is the most common thing to forget.
Area
Tap to flip
The amount of flat space enclosed by a shape, measured in square units (cm², m², km², etc.).
A 3 × 4 rectangle has area 12 cm². A circle's area formula is A = πr².
Pi (π)
Tap to flip
An irrational constant ≈ 3.14159… It is the ratio of any circle's circumference to its diameter.
π appears in both area (A = πr²) and circumference (C = 2πr) formulas.
Sector
Tap to flip
A "pie slice" region bounded by two radii and an arc. Its area is a fraction of the full circle's area.
A semicircle is a sector with angle 180°; a quarter-circle has angle 90°.

6Worked examples — applying A = πr²

Four types you need to master. Click Reveal next step on each example to see the working unfold.

Example A — Given the radius

Find the area of a circle with radius 5 cm. Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.

1
Identify what's given
We have the radius directly. No halving needed.
r = 5 cm  ⟹  use A = πr²
2
Substitute and calculate
Replace r with 5 and evaluate.
A = π × 5²
= π × 25
= 78.54 cm² (2 d.p.)
3
Sanity check with the 3r² rule
A quick rough estimate: A ≈ 3 × r². Compare it to the exact answer.
3 × 5² = 3 × 25 = 75 cm²
Sanity check passed: 78.54 is close to the estimate of 75. If the exact answer were wildly different (say 785 or 7.85), you would know immediately that an error had been made.
AnswerA = 78.54 cm²

Example B — Given the diameter (halve it first!)

A circular pizza has a diameter of 30 cm. Find its area, correct to 1 decimal place.

⚠ Common error — do not substitute the diameter directly into A = πr²
Using d = 30 in place of r gives A = π × 30² = 2827 cm² — this is four times too large. The formula uses the radius, so you must halve the diameter first. This is the single most common mistake on this topic.
1
Find the radius first
We are given the diameter. Divide by 2 before anything else.
r = d ÷ 2 = 30 ÷ 2 = 15 cm
2
Apply the formula with r = 15
Now substitute the correct value.
A = π × 15²
= π × 225
= 706.9 cm² (1 d.p.)
3
Sanity check
3r² = 3 × 225 = 675 cm². Our answer 706.9 cm² is close to this. ✓
Area of the pizzaA = 706.9 cm²

Example C — Finding the radius given the area

A circular garden has an area of 50.27 m². Find its radius, correct to 1 decimal place.

1
Set up the equation
We know A; we need r. Write the formula, substitute A = 50.27.
50.27 = πr²
2
Rearrange to isolate r²
Divide both sides by π.
r² = 50.27π ≈ 16
3
Take the positive square root
Radius must be positive.
r = √16 = 4.0 m
Radius of the gardenr = 4.0 m

Example D — Practical context: sprinkler coverage

A centre-pivot sprinkler rotates 360° and waters everything within 8 m of the centre. What area does it cover, to the nearest m²?

1
Identify the radius
The radius is given directly as 8 m. No halving needed.
r = 8 m
2
Apply the formula
Substitute r = 8 and evaluate.
A = π × 8²
= π × 64
≈ 201 m²
3
Sanity check and interpret
3 × 64 = 192 m². Our answer 201 m² is close. ✓
Sprinkler covers approximately201 m²
