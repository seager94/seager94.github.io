1Why this matters

A real situation that's secretly today's algebra.

You sign up for a streaming service. It charges a $5 set-up fee plus $12 per month. At the end of the year you check your total spend — it's $149.

You want to know: how many months have you actually been paying?

You could try every possibility from 1 to 12, but there's a faster way — set up an equation and solve it in a few neat steps.

Let m = number of months. Total cost: 12m + 5 = 149.

Today we learn to undo the operations in reverse order — subtract 5, then divide by 12 — to find m in two steps. We'll also extend this to equations where the coefficient of x is negative, and we'll always finish by substituting back to verify the answer.

2Key vocabulary

Tap each card to flip and reveal the definition. These five words are the language of solving equations.

Linear equation
Tap to flip
An equation where the variable has a power of 1 — no squares or cubes.
3x + 5 = 17 is linear. 3x2 + 5 = 17 is not.
Inverse operation
Tap to flip
The operation that undoes another. Addition ↔ Subtraction. Multiplication ↔ Division.
Undo "+ 5" with "− 5". Undo "× 3" with "÷ 3". Undo "× (−2)" with "÷ (−2)".
Coefficient
Tap to flip
The number that multiplies the variable. It can be positive or negative.
In −3x + 7 = 1, the coefficient of x is −3.
Solution
Tap to flip
The value of the variable that makes the equation true. The solution might be negative.
For −3x + 7 = 1, the solution is x = 2.
Verify
Tap to flip
Substitute your solution back into the original equation. If both sides are equal, the solution is confirmed.
Check x = 2 in −3x + 7 = 1: −3(2)+7 = −6+7 = 1 ✓

5Worked examples — inverse operations method

Three examples, each with three steps: identify → apply inverse operations → verify. Click "Reveal next step" to follow along.

Example A
Solve: 3x + 5 = 17
1
Identify the operations — then plan the inverse steps
x is multiplied by 3, then 5 is added. Undo in reverse: subtract 5 first, then divide by 3.
3x + 5 = 17
Step 1: undo +5 → subtract 5 from both sides.
Step 2: undo ×3 → divide both sides by 3.
2
Apply the inverse operations — one at a time
Subtract 5 from both sides, then divide both sides by 3.
3x + 5 = 17original
3x + 5 − 5 = 17 − 5subtract 5 both sides
3x = 12simplify
3x3 = 123divide by 3 both sides
x = 4simplify
3
Verify — substitute x = 4 into the original equation
Never skip this step. If LHS ≠ RHS, go back and find the error.
LHS = 3(4) + 5substitute x = 4
       = 12 + 5 = 17multiply, then add
RHS = 17
LHS = RHS ✓solution confirmed
Verified: both sides equal 17 when x = 4.
Answerx = 4
Example B
Solve: 4x − 9 = 11
1
Identify the operations and plan the inverse steps
x is multiplied by 4, then 9 is subtracted. Undo in reverse: add 9 first, then divide by 4.
4x − 9 = 11
Step 1: undo −9 → add 9 to both sides.
Step 2: undo ×4 → divide both sides by 4.
2
Apply the inverse operations
Add 9 to both sides, then divide both sides by 4.
4x − 9 = 11original
4x − 9 + 9 = 11 + 9add 9 both sides
4x = 20simplify
4x4 = 204divide by 4 both sides
x = 5simplify
3
Verify — substitute x = 5 into the original equation
LHS = 4(5) − 9substitute x = 5
       = 20 − 9 = 11multiply, then subtract
RHS = 11
LHS = RHS ✓solution confirmed
Verified: both sides equal 11 when x = 5.
Answerx = 5
Example C — negative coefficient of x
Solve: −3x + 2 = 14
1
Identify the operations and plan the inverse steps
x is multiplied by −3, then 2 is added. Undo in reverse: subtract 2 first, then divide by −3. Dividing by a negative flips the sign of the result.
−3x + 2 = 14
Step 1: undo +2 → subtract 2 from both sides.
Step 2: undo ×(−3) → divide both sides by −3.
2
Apply the inverse operations — watch the sign when dividing by −3
−3x + 2 = 14original
−3x + 2 − 2 = 14 − 2subtract 2 both sides
−3x = 12simplify
−3x−3 = 12−3divide by −3 both sides
x = −4positive ÷ negative = negative
3
Verify — substitute x = −4 into the original equation
Verification is especially important when the answer is negative — it catches sign errors instantly.
LHS = −3(−4) + 2substitute x = −4
       = 12 + 2 = 14negative × negative = positive
RHS = 14
LHS = RHS ✓solution confirmed
Verified: both sides equal 14 when x = −4. Notice that −3 × −4 = +12 — a negative times a negative is positive.
Answerx = −4
