7Two truths and a lie

Three claims about index laws. Two are true — one is sneakily false. Click each card to flip; spot the lie before you flip them all.

The lie was Claim C.

When raising a power to a power you multiply the exponents, not add them: (x3)2 = x3×2 = x6. Claim B applies only to multiplication of like-base terms (x3 × x2 = x5), which is what makes Claim C tempting — it looks like the rule we just used in Claim B.
