5Worked example — explain as you go

Solve 3x + 7 = 22 for x. After each step we'll ask you to type a one-sentence reason for what just happened. The next step won't unlock until you write something — even a rough guess is fine.

1
3x + 7 = 22
Before we touch this — what's the goal? In one line, what does it mean to "solve for x"?
To find the value of x that makes both sides equal.
2
3x = 15   (subtracted 7 from both sides)
Why did we subtract 7 from both sides — and not just the left?
To keep the equation balanced — whatever you do to one side you must do to the other.
3
x = 5   (divided both sides by 3)
Why divide by 3 here, rather than subtract 3?
3x means 3 times x — the inverse of multiplying is dividing, not subtracting.
4
Check: 3(5) + 7 = 15 + 7 = 22 ✓
In your own words, why is it worth doing this check at the end?
To catch arithmetic slips — if 5 makes both sides equal then it really is the solution.
