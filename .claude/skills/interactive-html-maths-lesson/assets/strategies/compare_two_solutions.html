8Two ways to the same answer

Two students were asked to calculate 15% of $80. Both got the right answer using different methods. Read both carefully, then answer the three questions below.

🛍️ A jacket is $80 with 15% off — how much is the discount?
Method A — Multiplier
15% as a decimal: 0.15
0.15 × 80
= 12
$12 off
Method B — Build up from 10%
10% of 80 = 8
5% of 80 = 4  (half of 10%)
15% = 10% + 5% = 8 + 4
= 12
$12 off
