1Which one doesn't belong?

There's no single right answer — every one is the odd one out for some reason. Pick the one you think doesn't belong, then read why each is defensible.

9 doesn't belong because…

It's the only single-digit number — the other three are two-digit. (Also the smallest.)

16 doesn't belong because…

It's the only even number. The other three (9, 25, 43) are odd.

25 doesn't belong because…

It's the only one ending in 5. (Or — it's the only one that's exactly halfway between two perfect squares: 16 = 4² and 36 = 6².)

43 doesn't belong because…

It's the only non-square. 9 = 3², 16 = 4², 25 = 5². 43 is also the only prime.
