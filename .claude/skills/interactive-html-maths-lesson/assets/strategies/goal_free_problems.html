11Open inquiry — what can you find out?

There's no question here. Just a shape with all its measurements labelled. Write down everything you can work out from the information given — areas, perimeters, angles, comparisons, anything. The more the better.

What can you find out?

Stuck? Some starter questions
  • What is the difference between the parallel sides?
  • What is the perimeter?
  • What is the area?
  • Is the trapezium isosceles?
  • How does the area compare to a rectangle of the same height?
  • What would happen to the area if you doubled the height?
