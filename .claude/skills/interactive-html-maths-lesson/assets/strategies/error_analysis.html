1 4x − 12 = 2x + 6

3 x = −3

✓ Right — that's where the mistake first appears.

Why: moving terms between sides flips the sign. From 4x − 12 = 2x + 6, when we add 12 to both sides and subtract 2x from both sides we should get 4x − 2x = 6 + 12, i.e. 2x = 18 — not 2x = −6. The student flipped only one sign instead of two.
